Physics: Oscillations

A quick introduction to the topic from the notes featured on this website. Read the key ideas, then test your understanding.

Key ideas at a glance

Periodic motionMotion that repeats after a definite interval of time.
Time period (T)Time taken to complete one oscillation.
Frequency (f)Number of oscillations completed per second.
Simple harmonic motionAcceleration is proportional to displacement and directed towards the mean position.
